Risk matrix scorer

Score hazards on the 5 by 5 matrix used in the risk assessment sheet: likelihood times severity, before and after controls. Add your own hazards, and the matrix and the totals update as you change them. The four starting rows are examples only.

Rows: likelihood (5 at the top) · Columns: severity · Badge: hazards in that cell (current risk)

Runs in your browser. Nothing is saved or sent. Likelihood and severity scales follow the sheet: 1 rare to 5 almost certain, and 1 minimal to 5 catastrophic. Scoring is a structured judgement, not a measurement, so the numbers are only as good as the thinking behind them.
