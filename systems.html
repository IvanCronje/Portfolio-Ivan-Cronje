Systems I built

Every one of these was built in-house, with spreadsheets, code and discipline, at zero licence cost. Each closes one gap I found when a department could not see what was happening. Together they form one loop, from a request arriving to a decision being made.

How they connect

One loop, eight steps

01 Request
Every request becomes a jobcard with a reference number.
02 Prioritise
Priority Index scores it. The system decides the order, not the loudest voice.
03 Plan
PM programme, AC filter cycle and daily checklist packs schedule the planned work.
04 Do
Technicians work by priority and by zone, in buddy pairs.
05 Close the loop
Guest follow-up tracker. A job is finished when the guest confirms.
06 Inspect
Room maintenance index and the facilities audit find what nobody reported.
07 Measure
Performance tracker, utilities and energy dashboards turn work into numbers.
08 Decide
Budget and capital calculator turn the numbers into investment decisions.
