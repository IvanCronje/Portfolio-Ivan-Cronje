Pump restart surge simulation

A power cut, a generator changeover and the booster pumps restart into a line that has lost pressure. This models the pressure spike that can follow, and shows why a staggered restart is the first fix. Pick a scenario, then change any number.

Concept · illustrative inputs, not validated against measured data
Pressure at the end of the run, shown against the rating of the weakest part (dashed). The shape is idealised and damped.

Model: Joukowsky surge. Wave speed a = sqrt((K/rho) / (1 + (K/E)(D/e))) with K = 2.2 GPa for water; surge = rho x a x change in velocity when the flow changes faster than 2L/a, and 2 x rho x L x change in velocity / time when it changes more slowly. It is an upper bound for a single straight run: it ignores pump curves, check valve dynamics, air pockets, friction and branches, and real peaks are usually lower. The 70% arrestor figure is an illustrative assumption, not a manufacturer rating. Runs in your browser; nothing is saved or sent.
