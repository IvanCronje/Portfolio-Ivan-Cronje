人物 · How I work

The building is the experience

That line is the thesis of the textbook I am writing, and it is also how I work. A guest does not buy a boiler or a chiller. They buy a shower that runs hot at six in the morning and a room that is quiet at two. Everything I build in a plant room is aimed at that.

I write everything down. Where I arrive, there is usually no register, no history and no schedule, so the first work is always the same: find out what is actually installed, record what it is doing, and turn that into a plan someone else can run without me in the room. Then I measure it, and I report what the numbers say rather than what would be comfortable.

記録

Record first

Asset registers, jobcards, readings and photographs before opinions. If it is not written down it did not happen.

測定

Measure, then argue

Consumption, litres, failure counts, rands. A capital motivation should be arithmetic a financial director can check.

正直

Report honestly

An audit at 56.92% is a starting line, not an embarrassment. Gaps get named, owned and dated.

錬

Train the team

SOPs, simulations and competency levels so the standard survives whoever is on shift.

Premier Resort Knysna – The Moorings. Department taken over May 2026 with no handover and rebuilt from a standing start.

Capital project · commissioned 2026 R619,403 EX VAT

Hot water plant replacement

Guests were waiting two to three minutes for hot water at the outlet. Identified, motivated, specified and delivered the main building hot water plant replacement — 2 × 30 kW modular air-to-water heat pumps and 2 × 2,500 litre heat accumulators — and built the case from first principles: 1,500 litres of existing storage against an estimated 7,500-litre demand for the 50-room main building, eight boiler element failures in twelve months and Legionella exposure from outlet temperatures not reached in time. Contracted at R712,313 including VAT, with Blygold coil protection specified against the coastal salt air.

Re-specification

Moved off the quoted R926,085 boiler option to heat pumps and accumulators, rebuilding an existing vessel back into supply.

Compliance framing

Escalated Legionella and water hygiene exposure as a compliance and trading risk, not a guest comfort issue.

Works supervised around a trading hotel with minimal guest disruption, then handed into structured planned maintenance.
