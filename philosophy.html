哲学 · Philosophy of hospitality

The building
is the experience

Hospitality is a promise of comfort, safety and care, delivered through a building. Engineering exists to keep that promise unbroken. Everything else I do, the registers, the schedules, the capital cases and the training, is technique. This page is the reason.

Eight principles
01

The building is the experience

A guest does not buy a boiler or a chiller. They buy a shower that runs hot at six in the morning and a room that is quiet at two. Everything I build in a plant room is aimed at that. Nobody books a hotel for the plumbing, but every reason they do book depends on what we look after.

整
02

Fixed is not finished

A job is finished when the guest is happy and we have checked. Fix it, test it, tell them, check back, log it. The follow-up is the part that separates a repair from a service.

仕上
03

Record first

Asset registers, jobcards, readings and photographs before opinions. Where I arrive there is usually no register, no history and no schedule, so the first work is always the same: find out what is installed, record what it is doing, and turn that into a plan someone else can run without me in the room.

記録
04

Measure, then argue

Consumption, litres, failure counts, rands. A capital motivation should be arithmetic a financial director can check. The hot water plant was not an opinion about comfort; it was 1,500 litres of storage against 7,500 litres of demand.

測定
05

Report honestly

An audit at 56.92% is a starting line, not an embarrassment. Gaps get named, owned and dated. I would rather show a manager a real number than a comfortable one.

正直
06

Our department

The shift is from “this is my department” to “this is our department”. The team owns problems and success together, and I stay accountable as the leader. We work as a team, fall as a team, and rise as a team.

共
07

Train the team

SOPs, simulations and competency levels so the standard survives whoever is on shift. A standard that lives in one person's head is not a standard. Training is written in-house, so it is taught rather than assumed.

錬
08

Prevent before you react

Planned work should outweigh reactive work. A property that only responds to faults is always one step behind its guests. Walk the building, find it before the guest does, and fix the cause rather than the symptom.

予防
共 · How the team works

We see it, we own it,
we fix it, we prevent it,
we improve it

See it Own it Fix it Prevent it Improve it

A department runs on rhythm, not heroics. These are the habits that keep the standard from depending on any one person, me included.

Monday
Focus
Set the week's priorities with the team, so everyone knows what matters before the first fault comes in.
Daily
Walk the building
Random walks through guest areas, so we find problems ourselves instead of waiting to be told.
Per project
War room
Before a big job starts, sit down and think it through, including how it lands on guests.
Friday
Review
What happened, what worked, what failed and what needs improving, written down and carried into next week.
空手道 · Where it comes from

Standards are kept,
not claimed

Karate is where the working method came from: repeat the basics until they hold under pressure, record what happened honestly, and treat standards as something you keep rather than something you claim. The same discipline that gets a technique right at the thousandth repetition gets a boiler room right at three in the morning.

Somewhere tonight a guest is lying awake. Make sure it is not because of us.
