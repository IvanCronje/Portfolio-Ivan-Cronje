Assumptions and limits
Method

Equivalent annual cost = capital recovery over the expected life, plus operating, maintenance and downtime costs per year. With a 0% rate, capital is spread evenly over the life. Options with different lives are compared on a per-year basis, so a longer-lived option is not penalised.

Limits

The two capital figures are the quoted amounts for the hot water plant (R926,085 boiler-based quote, R712,313 including VAT for the heat pump solution). Operating, maintenance and risk costs start at zero because they must come from real records. The capital gap on the quotes is a comparison of quoted figures, not a verified saving. Check scope, VAT and final costs before calling it one. This is a concept, not a validated model.
