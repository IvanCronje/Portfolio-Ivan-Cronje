Why old jobs must climb the queue

Three ways of choosing the next job: oldest first, highest score only, or score with age escalation. Feed the same random jobs to each and watch what happens to urgent work and to the jobs nobody asked about for a month.

Concept · illustrative inputs, not validated against measured data
Open jobs over time. A backlog that keeps climbing is work the team is not getting to.

Model: jobs arrive at random each day with a base score from 0 to 100 (urgent jobs score 75 and above), each needing a random number of hours; the team spends its daily capacity on the top of the queue, splitting a job across days if needed. Age escalation multiplies a job's score by 1 + days open / 30, capped at 3 times, as in the Priority Index. It ignores skills, parts, access windows, guest impact timing and emergencies that interrupt work. All inputs are illustrative. Runs in your browser; nothing is saved or sent.
