Does the roster cover the clock?

A hotel never closes, so engineering cover has to survive leave, sickness and days off. Set the size of the team, how many work nights and how often people are absent, and see how many days the roster really holds a safe level of cover.

Concept · illustrative inputs, not validated against measured data
Share of days with full cover, by roster size. Your chosen size is marked. Higher is better.

Model: each person works the share of days left after their days off, and is absent on a working day with the stated chance, independently. Day and night staff are separate pools, so a night worker cannot cover a day gap. 'Fully covered' means the day minimum and the night minimum are both met. It ignores skills, leaders, overtime, call-outs and the order of leave, so it understates what a smart roster can recover and overstates a random one. 400 random years per point, repeatable. Inputs are illustrative. Runs in your browser; nothing is saved or sent.
